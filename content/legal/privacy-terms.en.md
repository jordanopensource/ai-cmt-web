# Terms & Privacy Policy

mrsd is a content moderation tool built by the Jordan Open Source Association (JOSA). It helps Page administrators detect harmful comments on the Facebook Pages they manage and decide what to do about them.

By connecting a Page to mrsd, you agree to the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy below.

## Part 1: Terms of Use

### Who can use mrsd

You may connect a Facebook Page only if you are authorized to manage it. You are responsible for the actions you and your moderators take through mrsd.

### How mrsd works

mrsd reads the comments on the Pages you connect and uses an AI model to flag comments that may be harmful, such as harassment or hate speech. **A human always makes the final decision.** mrsd never hides, deletes or replies to a comment on its own; it only does so when you or your moderators choose to.

### AI limitations

The AI model can be wrong. It may flag acceptable comments or miss harmful ones, including satire, political speech or creative expression. Review flagged comments before acting on them.

### Acceptable use

Use mrsd only to moderate Pages you manage, in line with Meta's Terms and Community Standards. Don't use it to profile, track or target individual commenters beyond what moderation of your own Page requires.

### Pilot status

mrsd is currently in a pilot phase. Features may change, and the service may be unavailable at times. We will tell you before making significant changes to these terms.

## Part 2: Privacy Policy

### What we collect

We only collect data from the Pages you choose to connect. We do not collect data from Facebook users in general.

| Data | What it includes | Why we need it |
|---|---|---|
| Page details | Page name, ID, category, profile picture, follower count, public bio | So you can choose which Page to moderate and see it in mrsd |
| Page access credential | A secure access token issued by Meta | To read comments and carry out the actions you choose, on your behalf |
| Posts | Post text, link, publish date, engagement counts | To show which posts have comments that need attention |
| Comments | Comment text, time, likes, thread, hidden status | To detect harmful content and let you review it |
| Comment author details | Commenter's public Facebook name, public profile ID and profile picture | To give moderators context, such as repeated abuse from the same account |
| Moderation actions | Which action was taken, on which comment, by which mrsd user, and when | To keep an accountability record |

**We do not collect:** private messages, friends lists, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or any data from outside the Pages you connect.

### Facebook permissions we request

| Permission | What it lets mrsd do |
|---|---|
| `pages_show_list` | List the Pages you manage so you can pick one |
| `business_management` | Confirm the connection through JOSA's verified Meta business account |
| `pages_read_engagement` | Read your Page's details and engagement |
| `pages_read_user_content` | Read posts and comments on your Page |
| `pages_manage_engagement` | Hide, unhide, delete or reply to comments when you choose to |
| `pages_manage_metadata` | Receive new comments in real time |

mrsd does not request access to your Page inbox, ads, or your personal Facebook profile, and **never publishes new posts on your Page**.

### How we use your data

We use this data only to detect harmful comments, show them to you for review, carry out the moderation actions you choose, and keep a record of those actions. We don't use it for advertising.

### Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with advertisers, data brokers or anyone outside the moderation workflow. Data moves only between Meta and mrsd's own servers.

### How long we keep data

- **Access credentials:** only while your Page is connected. Deleted immediately when you disconnect or revoke access.
- **Posts and comments:** only while they still exist on Facebook. If a comment is deleted on Facebook, we delete our copy.
- **AI classifications:** kept only alongside the moderation record they relate to.
- **Moderation records:** kept as an accountability record, even after the original comment is gone.

### Security

- Access credentials are stored on our servers only and are never sent to your browser.
- Only signed-in mrsd users with a moderator role can take moderation actions, and every action is recorded.

### Your choices and rights

- **Disconnect anytime.** Disconnecting a Page stops all data collection from it. You can also ask us to delete the comments we stored from that Page. The moderation record is kept.
- **If you commented on a connected Page,** you can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it. We will delete it, except the record of any moderation action already taken on your comment.
